High Resolution Imager for the Habitable Worlds Observatory

We are a UK-led team of universities and national laboratories, with international partners, developing concepts and technology for the main camera on NASA's next flagship space telescope. Our work is funded by the UK Space Agency.

The mission

The Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO) is a large space telescope in development at NASA, planned for launch in the 2040s. It is the first mission designed to search for signs of life on planets around nearby stars, and will also carry forward the broad astrophysics of Hubble and JWST.

The instrument

The High Resolution Imager (HRI) is planned as HWO's main camera and a general-purpose instrument for the wider astronomy community. The baseline instrument images a 3′ × 2′ field at wavelengths from 200 to 2200 nm through more than fifty filters, with grisms for slitless spectroscopy and astrometry at sub-microarcsecond precision. This supports science across exoplanets, from measuring the masses of potentially habitable planets found by HWO's coronagraph to probing the atmospheres of transiting planets; the Solar System, from ocean worlds such as Europa and Enceladus to the giant planets and planetary defence; and astrophysics and cosmology, from resolved stars in nearby galaxies to the nature of dark matter and the expansion rate of the Universe.

In our design concepts, a dichroic splits the light into ultraviolet–visible and near-infrared arms that observe simultaneously, each with its own filter wheels, grisms and detector mosaic, and windowed readout enables fast cadences for bright or time-variable targets. A separate channel, developed with our partners at CEA, provides the astrometry, and HRI also supports the observatory's fine guidance and wavefront sensing. We are also studying an additional far-ultraviolet channel. Details are given in our publications.

What we do

Instrument concept

End-to-end design studies for HRI, developed in step with NASA's evolving observatory architecture.

Technology development

Advancing the readiness of detectors, optics and other key technologies, drawing on UK space-instrument heritage.

Science

Translating HWO science, from exoplanets and the Solar System to cosmology, into instrument requirements.

Partnerships

Working with NASA and with UK and international partners towards a future HRI instrument team.

Industry

A UK-led High Resolution Imager would be built in partnership with industry.

The UK has world-class capability in space detectors, precision optics and aerospace engineering. We work with UK companies from the earliest design stages, so that the instrument draws on their technology and they are positioned to contribute to its construction.

Companies interested in contributing are welcome to get in touch.

Team

Our team brings together UK groups with heritage on space instruments for missions including JWST, Euclid and Ariel, with a French partner contributing astrometry expertise. Together we bring scientists and engineers from across the instrument lifecycle.
